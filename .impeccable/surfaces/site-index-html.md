## Scope

BEZETA Barberstudio one-page site (site/index.html). Mode: Persuade. Spanish. Visitor arrives mostly on a phone from social media, decides in seconds, books on Booksy (URL pending) or finds the shop.

## Content and constraints

Real: logo, name, positioning (urban technique + classic finish). Pending from owner: address, hours, photos, socials, Booksy URL, confirmed services. No invented prices, reviews or stats. Placeholders visibly marked and centralised in one config object.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a head of hair being designed: rayas are carved into a dense black hair texture, and every section is opened by a cut. Refuses the category default of black-and-gold luxe hero photo plus three service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Pure black and pure white only, no greys; tone comes from the density of fine hair strokes (raise from Riley moiré: contrast generated optically by line frequency, never by mixed greys). Carved lines are crisp white channels at the logo's slash angle. Type: Archivo at heavy expanded widths for display (echoing the B/Z monogram), Archivo condensed caps for labels, Bodoni Moda only as a rare serif echo of the BEZETA wordmark.

STORY: Visitor sees the shop cuts with precision, understands it blends street technique and classic finish, sees the work, finds hours/location, books.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed hair-grain field. A diagonal raya carves across the screen on load at the logo's slash angle, revealing the BEZETA logo left of centre at large scale, headline below it, "Reservar cita" primary and "Horario y ubicación" secondary under it; nav strip with B/Z mark and Reservar top right. On mobile a sticky bottom Reservar bar.

FORM: Grounded candidate 4 of 7 ("Las rayas" — hair-design line carving), seed: d49c15b171ff4236. Signature interaction: pointer drags carve temporary rayas into the hero hair that slowly grow back; scroll-scrubbed rayas carve in to open each section. Motion grammar (raise from desert neon sign program: every lamp numbered): every cut is sequenced and timed in a fixed order, never scattered.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
