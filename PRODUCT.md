# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS (single page, no build step) — fast, free to host (GitHub Pages / Netlify / Vercel), easy for a beginner owner to maintain.

## Users

People looking for a barber nearby who discover BEZETA via social media or search, mostly on their phone. They want to judge the shop's quality and vibe in seconds, then find where it is, when it's open, and book.

## Product Purpose

Website for BEZETA Barberstudio, a barbershop owned by the user's friend. Success = visitors trust the shop's level and go to book (via Booksy) or visit.

## Positioning

A blend of modern urban barbering (fades, designs, beard work) with the care and finish of a classic barbershop.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Booking happens on Booksy. A "Reservar" button linking to the owner's Booksy page will be added later; the Booksy URL is not yet known.
- Language: Spanish.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: BEZETA Barberstudio.
- Logo (site/assets/logo-original.jpg): white on black. Bold sans "B/Z" monogram split by a diagonal slash (razor-cut feel); high-contrast serif "BEZETA" wordmark with a barber-pole glyph; "BARBERSTUDIO" spaced caps between rules. Monochrome identity.

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo only (raster JPG).
- Owner has address, opening hours, photos and social media accounts — not yet provided. Do not invent address, hours, prices, services list, reviews or testimonials.

## Product Principles

1. Show the level in the first seconds: the site itself must feel as sharp as the cuts.
2. Booking is always one tap away.
3. Mobile first: most visitors arrive from social media on a phone.
4. Real over invented: only real photos, real info, no fake reviews.
